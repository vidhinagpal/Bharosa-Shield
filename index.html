Bharosa Shield A1–A6 Multi-Agent Architecture

A pipeline of six specialized autonomous agents collaborating with mathematical precision to protect vulnerable citizens.

A1: Input Quality & Extraction
Extraction Gate

Checks whether submitted content is readable and extracts information reliably.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
  • Checks image clarity, blur and lighting
  • Uses OCR to extract document text
  • Extracts text from messages and voice notes
  • Detects missing or incomplete information
OUTPUT: Extracted text + quality assessment
A2: Threat Pattern Detection
Pattern Engine

Identifies known fraud indicators and suspicious patterns in message content.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
  • Detects OTP, PIN and password requests
  • Identifies suspicious URLs (.apk, typosquats, shorteners)
  • Detects urgency and account-blocking threats
  • Flags upfront payment demands and impersonation patterns
OUTPUT: Threat indicators + risk findings
A3: Linguistic & Semantic Analysis
Semantic NLP

Understands what the message or document actually means beneath the surface.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
  • Understands message intent and context
  • Identifies emotional manipulation (fear, greed, urgency, isolation)
  • Analyzes regional-language content (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali)
  • Explains complex financial clauses in simple language
OUTPUT: Semantic interpretation + suspicious intent
A4: Contextual Verification
Forensic Reality

Checks whether claims and identities are consistent with trusted institutional standards.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
  • Examines claimed sender identity (TRAI headers vs personal mobile SIMs)
  • Checks claimed organization against trusted references (RBI, SERC, DoT)
  • Identifies inconsistent offer details
  • Compares information with available trusted sources
OUTPUT: Verification findings + unknown information
A5: Autonomous Safety Gatekeeper
Invariant Guard

Prevents unsafe or unsupported conclusions and enforces zero-compromise safety invariants.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
  • Checks disagreements between agents and arbitrates conflicts
  • Prevents unsupported safe verdicts (Zero Blind Signatures Policy)
  • Escalates high-risk or uncertain cases (Veto on OTP / UPI PIN demands)
  • Enforces privacy and safety restrictions (PII scrubbing)
OUTPUT: Approved, blocked or escalated assessment
A6: Decision Coordinator & Action
Synthesis & Voice

Combines all findings and communicates the final result clearly to vulnerable citizens.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
  • Aggregates evidence from all agents (A1 to A5)
  • Produces SCAM, RISKY or NO KNOWN RED FLAGS verdict
  • Generates a simple-language explanation without legal jargon
  • Recommends one clear action and prepares multilingual voice output
OUTPUT: Final verdict + explanation + recommended action
